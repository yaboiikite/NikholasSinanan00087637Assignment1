My quick thoughts on Ninja Kiwi's long awaited new project: SAS5

The game is definitly different from the long running SAS4, which has been the developers prize for near a decade. It has its improvments & its "lacking" aspects, the game isnt anywhere near as chilling grimey & dark as SAS4 but it has a better feel to it & a better upgrade & class system. The rng is worse but I prefer the new gameplay in all honesty.
However to end of this short dedicated review, the game is barely a week old & has a lot of room for potential & I do hope the developers pull through with how promising things look so far.

Voltaic Bolter Pistol Morlok Standard low teir energy pistol nothing noteworthy or too special outside the fact this is your first element based weapon found at the begining of the game Very good for crossing distances as the lightweight nature of the weapon has little to no movement penalties
CM 273 Pulsar Pistol Critical Mass Low to mid tier pistol that leans toward dps over crowd clearing capabilities relativly a rarer weapon Charge mechanic & high peirce makes it a fun weapon to use on tougher zombies
Galvanizer SMG Morlok Low tier starter weapon not much use outside of "this is all I have right now" Very lighweight & forgiving on the movement penalties, not meant for heavy class but is surely promising for others
Ronson 77-A Rifle Ronson A mid tier weapon for level 20 & below all around good stats while lacking an element Features a burst mechanic
HMV 040 Shotgun HMV A low tier starter shotgun but very reliable good for crowd clearing & commendable dps Features a 100% flinch chance stacked on top of a 50% knock back chance
Ronson KR-3182 Shotgun Ronson A proper mid tier weapon featuring the cryo element & a massive slow of 70% Incredible crowd control
Dominator Shotgun Shotlite A very good mid teir weapon with no featured element that has all around utility Features a clip system rather than a shell by shell reload which feels nicer than it sounds
Hiks M450 LMG Hiks A tricky low to mid teir lmg that features no element high peirce & high damage but low fire rate Features a ridiculous magazine of 110 & a range of 21 meters
CM 458 Polaris Rifle Critical Mass A mid to high tier early game energy weapon that features exemplary crowd clearing capabilities Featurs extremely high peirce & has a mechanic where bullets home in on targets
RIA T25 Igniter Rifle Rubicon Industries A solid mid tier weapon pickup featuring thermal ammo commendable peirce & solid DOT A high 50% flinch chance for a weapon of this type
Shiden SMG Shotlite An extremly good weapon, mid to high tier that boasts an energy element & lightweight nature Features the highest dps found on a weapon thus far & an accuracy mechanic making it the most accurate weapon in the early game
